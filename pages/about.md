House & Habit is a lifestyle magazine about the places we live and the people we invite into them. It covers home decor and interiors, hosting and entertaining, and the cozy side of everyday style.

It is written by Vallari Gadre, who spent a decade planning and buying for fashion and home-adjacent retail brands. That means a lot of time deciding which products deserve shelf space and which do not, a habit that carries straight into every edit on this site.

## What we promise

- **Seasonal and useful.** Every story ties to what is happening now, from fall decor to holiday hosting, and gives you something you can actually do this week.
- **Edited, not endless.** A short list of finds per story, chosen for quality, reviews and value.
- **Honest about affiliate links.** Some links earn a small commission. It never changes the price you pay, and it is always disclosed.
