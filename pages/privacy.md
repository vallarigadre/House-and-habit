This page explains what information House & Habit collects when you visit.

## Newsletter sign-ups

If you join the Sunday letter, your email address is stored securely by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, and used only to send you the newsletter. You can ask to be removed at any time by replying to any letter.

## Affiliate links and cookies

Product links to Amazon.com may place a cookie in your browser so Amazon can credit this site if you make a purchase. That cookie is set and managed by Amazon under its own privacy policy.

## Advertising

House & Habit may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show ads to you based on those visits.

You can turn off personalized advertising in [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), or opt out of many third-party vendors' cookies at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/). To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

## Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics and its own privacy-friendly counter to see which pages are read and where visitors come from. These counts are aggregated and are not used to identify you.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the [contact page](/contact/) and choose "Privacy or my data". We will get back to you within two working days.
