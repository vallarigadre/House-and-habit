December has a way of filling up before you notice. One week it is quiet, and the next you have a cocktail party on Friday, the office party on Wednesday, dinner at your sister's on Christmas Eve and a promise to walk the neighborhood lights with friends.

Each of those invitations asks for something different. The cocktail party wants a little sparkle. Family dinner wants comfort that still looks pulled together. The office party wants festive without trying too hard. Christmas morning wants the softest thing you own.

The trick that makes it easy: plan every outfit from the ground up. Pick the shoes and the bag first, because they are what carry you through the night, and they are the pieces you will wear again and again. The clothes simply follow.

Here are five Christmas outfit ideas, one for each kind of holiday plan, with exactly what to wear and why it works.

## How to plan your Christmas outfits this year

Pinterest Predicts named a return to glamour as one of its big ideas for the year ahead: richer fabrics, sculpted shoulders, bolder jewelry and the comeback of the brooch. For real life, that translates into three easy moves.

- **Velvet and satin, in deep colors.** Forest green, wine, chocolate and black read festive without a single sequin.
- **One heirloom-style detail.** A pearl clutch, a bow brooch or a gold chain strap does the work of a whole new dress.
- **Shoes you can stand in for three hours.** A block heel, a low pump, a loafer or a boot. Comfort is the new dressed up.

Before you shop, list your December plans and match each one to an outfit below. Most women need only two or three new pieces to cover the whole month.

## Outfit 2: What to wear to a family Christmas dinner

Family dinner means a long table, a warm kitchen, kids underfoot and at least one round of photos by the tree. You want to look lovely, sit comfortably for three hours and still be able to help clear the plates.

### The shoes: knee-high boots

The [Franco Sarto Balin knee-high boot](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B093VNWC9J?tag=creativedi03c-20) is the hero here. A tall boot under a midi skirt keeps you warm on the drive and the doorstep, and it looks polished without the ache of a heel. Choose a cognac or brown shade with cream and chocolate, or black with deep green.

### The bag: a quilted chain crossbody

Bring a [quilted crossbody with a convertible chain strap and twist lock](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B6H4Q6KH?tag=creativedi03c-20). It holds your phone, lipstick and car keys, leaves your hands free for the casserole dish and looks just a little dressy on the chain.

### The rest of the outfit

- A [high-waisted satin A-line midi skirt in coffee brown](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GVDWXZK4?tag=creativedi03c-20)
- A cream cable knit or a fine turtleneck tucked in at the front
- Small gold hoops and a velvet hair bow if you like a romantic touch

**Why it works:** Satin and knit is the coziest version of dressed up, and the skirt has room for second helpings. The boots and bag carry it from the table to the sofa to the drive home.

## Outfit 3: What to wear to the office holiday party

Office parties are often straight after work, which means the outfit has to start at your desk at 9 am and still feel festive at 6 pm. The secret is one swap: velvet on top.

### The shoes: classic loafers

The [Sam Edelman Loraine loafer](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08N3D7TF7?tag=creativedi03c-20) is the shoe you can wear all day, through a commute and a standing party with canapés. Polished, comfortable and work-appropriate, it keeps the velvet from feeling too formal.

### The bag: a structured work tote

A [structured leather-look work tote with compartments](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0G1T5WX3C?tag=creativedi03c-20) holds your laptop by day and a small pouch with lipstick, perfume and earrings for the five-minute switch before the party. A structured shape always looks more put together than a slouchy bag.

### The rest of the outfit

- An [open-front velvet blazer](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HM2W639F?tag=creativedi03c-20) in black, wine or green
- Black or chocolate wide-leg trousers
- A silky camisole or a fine knit underneath
- Your festive swap: statement earrings, or move the bow brooch from Outfit 1 onto the lapel

**Why it works:** It is office-proof and still unmistakably holiday. Take the blazer off for the Monday meeting, and the trousers and loafers are business as usual.

## Outfit 4: What to wear on Christmas morning

Christmas morning outfits get photographed more than any other, usually in bright window light and usually before coffee. The goal is matching, soft and easy to cook brunch in.

### The shoes: memory foam slippers

Start with real slippers, not socks on a cold floor. The [RockDove Nomad slipper with memory foam](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08CTFPBN5?tag=creativedi03c-20) has a cushioned footbed for a long morning of unwrapping, making pancakes and refilling coffee. Give each family member a pair on Christmas Eve and you have a new tradition.

### The bag: none needed

Christmas morning is the one time this month you can skip the bag. Put a little basket by the tree for gift tags, scissors, tape and a trash bag for paper, and you have everything you need.

### The rest of the outfit

- A [LILLUSORY two-piece knit sweater set](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D872D7YN?tag=creativedi03c-20) in oatmeal, cream or a soft heather
- A chunky robe or long cardigan layered over the top
- Hair in a claw clip, tiny gold studs

**Why it works:** A matching knit set looks intentional in photos and feels like pajamas. If the in-laws arrive for lunch, add the loafers from Outfit 3 and you are dressed.

## Your Christmas outfit checklist

Use this the week before each party so nothing catches you off guard.

1. **Match the plan to the outfit.** Write each December date next to Outfits 1 to 5.
2. **Test the shoes now.** Wear new heels or boots around the house for an hour this week.
3. **Pack the bag the night before.** Phone, card, lipstick, a blister bandage and a mini perfume.
4. **Check the coat.** Make sure your coat length works with your skirt or dress.
5. **Order early.** Place Amazon orders by early December so exchanges arrive before Christmas.
