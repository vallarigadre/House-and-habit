Every Thanksgiving host knows the moment. Guests arrive at two, the turkey is promising three thirty, and the kitchen fills with hungry people who want to help but mostly want to snack. ## Why the appetizer table matters on Thanksgiving

Thanksgiving is a long day of eating, and the hour before dinner sets the tone. A good appetizer table does three jobs at once. It keeps hunger in check so nobody fills up on rolls, it pulls guests out of the kitchen, and it gives early arrivals something to do with their hands besides asking where the serving spoons are.

The rule is to keep it light enough that people still have room for the main event. Think salty, crunchy, tangy and fresh, in small portions. Plan on about four to six bites per person if dinner follows within two hours, and a little more if it does not.

The second rule is that nothing on this table should need you once guests arrive. Everything below can be made one to three days ahead, and the board itself goes together in about thirty minutes.

### Shopping list for 10 to 12 guests

**Cheese (about 2 pounds total):**

- 1 wedge aged white cheddar
- 1 small wheel of brie or camembert
- 1 wedge of Gouda, aged or smoked
- 1 log of goat cheese, rolled in chopped dried cranberries and pecans
- 1 wedge of a blue cheese such as Gorgonzola dolce

**Meat (about 1 pound total):**

- Prosciutto, sliced thin
- Genoa salami or sopressata
- A small piece of dry chorizo or summer sausage for slicing

**Fall fruit:**

- 2 red pears, sliced just before serving and tossed with a little lemon juice
- 1 bunch red grapes, cut into small clusters
- 6 to 8 fresh figs, halved (or dried figs if fresh are gone)
- 1 pomegranate, broken into pieces
- 1 cup dried apricots

**Sweet and tangy:**

- 1 cup cranberry sauce or cranberry relish
- 1 jar fig jam or apple chutney
- Honey for drizzling over the blue cheese
- Cornichons or pickled red onions

**Crunch:**

- 2 boxes of crackers in different shapes (one plain, one seeded or rosemary)
- 1 baguette, sliced and toasted into crostini
- Candied pecans, Marcona almonds or spiced walnuts

**Garnish:** fresh rosemary, sage leaves and thyme sprigs.

### Build order

1. **Bowls first.** Place three or four small bowls on the board for the cranberry sauce, chutney, cornichons and nuts. Little ceramic cups like the [LE TAUCI Sauce Cups 2 oz, Set of 6](https://link.amazon/B0eDVJ7dF) are the right size and keep wet things from soaking the crackers.
2. **Cheese next.** Spread the cheeses out so each sits in its own zone. Cut a few slices or wedges from each so guests know how to start. Set the brie whole with a knife beside it.
3. **Then the meat.** Fold prosciutto into loose ribbons, fan the salami into rivers that curve between the cheeses, and stack sliced chorizo in a small pile.
4. **Fruit in clusters.** Tuck grapes, figs and pears into the bigger gaps, keeping each fruit together rather than scattering it.
5. **Crackers last.** Fan crackers and crostini along the edges and beside the soft cheeses. Keep backup crackers in the box to refill.
6. **Fill every gap.** Pour nuts and dried apricots into the remaining spaces, then tuck in herbs and scatter pomegranate seeds over the top.
7. **Finish.** Drizzle honey over the blue cheese, set out a small honey pot such as the [HAPPI STUDIO Honey Jar with Dipper](https://link.amazon/B01klkpTN), and add cheese knives. Take the cheese out of the fridge about an hour before guests arrive so it tastes like itself. You can build the board up to two hours ahead, cover it loosely with plastic wrap and leave the crackers off until the last minute.

## 8 make-ahead Thanksgiving appetizers

Set two or three of these around the board, or make all eight for a bigger crowd. Each one holds well, and most taste better after a night in the fridge.

![An overhead spread of make-ahead Thanksgiving appetizers on oatmeal linen: cranberry brie bites, bacon-wrapped dates, whipped feta with pistachios, deviled eggs, antipasto skewers, pumpkin hummus, candied pecans and sausage stuffing balls](/images/thanksgiving-appetizers-3.jpg)

### 1. Cranberry brie bites

Cut puff pastry into 24 small squares and press them into a mini muffin tin. Add a cube of brie and a teaspoon of cranberry sauce to each. Bake at 375°F for about 15 minutes, until puffed and golden. **Make ahead:** assemble a day early, refrigerate in the tin, and bake just before guests arrive.

### 2. Whipped feta with hot honey

Blend 8 ounces of feta with 4 ounces of cream cheese and a squeeze of lemon until silky. Spread in a shallow bowl, drizzle with hot honey and finish with chopped pistachios and thyme. Serve with pita chips. **Make ahead:** whip up to three days early, top just before serving.

### 3. Bacon-wrapped dates

Stuff 20 pitted Medjool dates with a little goat cheese or an almond, wrap each in half a slice of bacon and secure with a pick. Bake at 400°F for 18 to 20 minutes, turning once. **Make ahead:** wrap them a day early, bake when you need them. Reusable metal skewers like these [Stainless Steel Cocktail Picks, 60 Pack](https://link.amazon/B02W57j4u) go straight into the oven and look far nicer than toothpicks.

### 4. Pumpkin hummus

Blend one can of chickpeas, one cup of pumpkin purée, two tablespoons of tahini, one garlic clove, lemon juice, cumin and a pinch of cinnamon. Top with olive oil and toasted pepitas. Serve with carrots, endive and crackers. **Make ahead:** up to three days.

### 5. Antipasto skewers

Thread a cherry tomato, a folded basil leaf, a mozzarella pearl, a folded slice of salami and a marinated artichoke quarter onto small skewers. Drizzle with balsamic glaze just before serving. **Make ahead:** assemble the morning of, cover and chill.

### 6. Rosemary candied pecans

Toss 3 cups of pecans with one beaten egg white, half a cup of brown sugar, a teaspoon of salt, chopped rosemary and a pinch of cayenne. Spread on a lined sheet and bake at 300°F for 30 minutes, stirring twice. **Make ahead:** up to a week, stored airtight. These also go on the board.

### 7. Deviled eggs with crispy shallots

Halve a dozen hard-boiled eggs, mash the yolks with mayonnaise, Dijon, a splash of pickle juice, salt and pepper, and pipe back into the whites. Top with store-bought crispy shallots and chives. **Make ahead:** cook and peel the eggs two days early, fill the morning of.

### 8. Sausage and sage stuffing balls

Mix one pound of breakfast sausage with three cups of dry stuffing cubes, one cup of shredded cheddar, one egg, chopped sage and enough broth to bind. Roll into balls and bake at 375°F for 20 to 25 minutes. Serve with cranberry sauce for dipping. **Make ahead:** roll and freeze up to two weeks ahead, bake from frozen with a few extra minutes.

## How to style the appetizer table

Once the food is ready, the setup is what makes it feel abundant. Pick a spot away from the kitchen, like a console, sideboard or the coffee table, so guests have a reason to move.

Set a pile of cloth napkins beside them; the [LA Linen 10-Pack Poly Poplin Napkin in Rust](https://link.amazon/B03LGDEH3) adds a warm fall color and goes straight into the wash.

**Add the season.** Scatter a few small pumpkins, pears and a sprig or two of eucalyptus around the board, and add low candles. Keep flames well away from the napkins and the reach of anyone grabbing a cracker.

**Set a small trash bowl.** One tiny bowl for pits, picks and fig stems keeps the board looking fresh for hours.

## Your make-ahead appetizer timeline

- **Two weeks before:** make and freeze the sausage stuffing balls. Order any serving pieces you need.
- **One week before:** make the rosemary candied pecans. Write your shopping list using the board list above.
- **Three days before:** shop for cheese, meat and crackers. Make the pumpkin hummus and whipped feta.
- **Two days before:** hard-boil and peel the eggs. Toast the crostini and store them airtight.
- **The day before:** assemble the brie bites and bacon-wrapped dates and refrigerate. Set out the board, bowls, plates, labels and napkins so the table is staged.
- **Morning of:** fill the deviled eggs, thread the antipasto skewers and slice the summer sausage.
- **One hour before guests:** take the cheese out, build the board and bake the brie bites, dates and stuffing balls.
- **As the doorbell rings:** add crackers, drizzle the honey and light the candles.
