Every fall there is a moment when the closet suddenly feels wrong. The summer pieces are too thin, last year's sweaters look tired and you have a long list of things you "need". The good news: you do not need much, and it does not have to cost much either.

A capsule wardrobe is a small set of pieces that all work together, so a handful of items turns into weeks of outfits. ## How this capsule works

Three simple rules keep a small wardrobe from feeling repetitive:

1. **Pick one palette.** Every piece here sits in the same warm family, so anything goes with anything.
2. **Mix textures, not just colors.** A cable knit, a fuzzy cardigan, brushed flannel and denim give you variety without needing more pieces.
3. **Spend a little more on the outer layer.** Jackets and boots are seen most and worn hardest, so they get the biggest share of the budget, still under $50 each.

## Your fall capsule checklist

- One or two thin layers: a fine turtleneck and a flannel shirt
- Two or three cozy sweaters in your palette
- One soft cardigan
- One pair of jeans in a light color
- One dress for dinners and holidays
- One jacket for mild days and one for cold days
- One dressy pair of boots and one practical pair
