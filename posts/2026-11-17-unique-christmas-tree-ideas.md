Scroll Instagram this month and you will see the same trees again and again: the all-red tree, the all-white tree, the all-gold tree. They are lovely, but they are not new. Pinterest tells a more interesting story. The trees people are saving right now mix colors nobody would have paired five years ago, borrow from opera houses and old circus posters, and treat ribbon as the main event instead of an afterthought.

Here are seven unique Christmas tree ideas that feel fresh this year, each with its palette and the exact ornaments, ribbon, topper and skirt, so you can recreate one in an afternoon. At the end: how to light any of them and the order to decorate in.

## 1. The cherry cola tree

**Palette:** glossy cherry red, deep burgundy, chocolate brown and a little antique gold.

This is the warm, slightly retro cousin of the classic red tree, and it is everywhere on Pinterest boards this season. The brown is what makes it: it turns a predictable red tree into something rich and grown-up, like a vintage soda sign by firelight.

- **Ornaments:** glossy cherry red balls, plus [matte chocolate brown glass ornaments](https://link.amazon/B0azHPcDv) tucked deep into the branches. Add a few small gold pieces for sparkle, no more than one in five.
- **Ribbon:** a [burgundy wired velvet ribbon](https://link.amazon/B09CuqF7S), cut into long tails and cascaded from top to bottom.
- **Topper:** a big burgundy velvet bow with tails that run halfway down the tree.
- **Skirt:** a chocolate brown faux fur or velvet skirt.
- **Lights:** warm white only. Cool white makes red look pink.

## 2. The opera tree

**Palette:** burgundy, black, antique gold and blush.

Pinterest named the Opera Aesthetic one of its big celebration trends for 2026, with searches for masquerade decor up 95% on last year. On a tree, that means drama: velvet, roses, candlelight and a skirt that looks like a stage curtain.

- **Ornaments:** [burgundy velvet ball ornaments](https://link.amazon/B0gRNkNjS) as your base, a handful of black glass balls, and antique gold filigree pieces. Tuck in faux red roses or dried amaranthus between the branches.
- **Ribbon:** wide black or burgundy velvet, draped in deep swags like theater drapery instead of tails.
- **Topper:** a gold crown, a fan of black feathers, or a vintage masquerade mask wired to the top.
- **Skirt:** yards of burgundy velvet or blush tulle, gathered and pooled around the base like a ball gown.
- **Around it:** a brass candelabra on a side table with [real wax LED taper candles](https://link.amazon/B09NkiNdI), which flicker like the real thing and are safe near branches and gifts.

## 3. The big bow tree

**Palette:** one ribbon color plus green. Red is classic; cream, pink or burgundy all work.

This one is almost too easy. Instead of ornaments, the tree is covered in oversized bows, set out evenly from top to bottom. Statement bows are on nearly every tree trend list this year, and it is easy to see why: it looks expensive and takes about 30 minutes.

- **Bows:** two packs of [large red velvet bows](https://link.amazon/B0c5JU14K). A 7-foot tree takes 18 to 24, set out in a loose diamond pattern so no two bows line up.
- **Ornaments:** optional. If you add any, use small balls in the same color as the bows, tucked deep.
- **Topper:** the biggest bow of all, with tails long enough to trail down the front.
- **Skirt:** plain cream or white, so the bows stand out.
- **Tip:** wire each bow to a sturdy inner branch, then fluff the loops and pull the tails so they point down and slightly out.

## 4. The romantasy tree

**Palette:** emerald, deep plum, midnight blue and antique gold.

Fantasy novels have taken over book clubs and TikTok, and now they are on the tree. Think enchanted forest: jewel tones, moons and stars, and a glow like lantern light.

- **Ornaments:** [plum purple velvet ball ornaments](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H7BJMMKM?tag=creativedi03c-20), emerald and midnight blue glass balls, and [gold moon and star ornaments](https://link.amazon/B09Z5tBfF). Add a few woodland pieces: a gold deer, a mushroom, an owl.
- **Ribbon:** a [dark green wired velvet ribbon](https://link.amazon/B0bh9sEtR), woven in and out of the branches.
- **Topper:** a gold starburst or a crescent moon.
- **Skirt:** deep green velvet, with a few lanterns with LED candles set around the base.

## 5. The neo deco tree

**Palette:** black, white, champagne gold and brass.

Pinterest's 2026 report also called out Neo Deco, a modern take on 1920s Art Deco with chevrons, fan shapes and shiny metal. On a tree, it is sleek and graphic, and it looks especially good on a slim tree in a city apartment.

- **Ornaments:** [black, white and gold Art Deco ball ornaments](https://link.amazon/B00QE2NX3) with geometric patterns, plus mirrored and brass balls.
- **Garland:** gold bead garland or strands of faux pearls, draped in shallow, even swags.
- **Topper:** a gold fan or a starburst with straight, sharp rays.
- **Skirt:** a black velvet skirt, or a brass tree collar for a polished finish.

## 6. The candy stripe tree

**Palette:** red and white, with a touch of green.

Searches for "circus interior" are up 130% on Pinterest, part of a playful trend it calls FunHaus. The holiday version is a candy stripe tree: cheerful, graphic and a little nostalgic, perfect for a kitchen or a family room.

- **Ornaments:** [red and white striped peppermint ball ornaments](https://link.amazon/B09sMMMLc), white pom poms, red glass balls and a few striped paper fans.
- **Ribbon:** red and white striped ribbon, tied into small bows or woven in spirals.
- **Topper:** a red and white striped star, or a small tent-shaped paper topper.
- **Skirt:** a red and white striped or ticking stripe skirt.
- **Lights:** warm white minis, plus a few big globe bulbs for a carnival feel.

## 7. The baby blue and chocolate tree

**Palette:** soft baby blue, chocolate brown and cream.

One of the most unexpected color pairings this year, and one of the prettiest. The brown keeps the blue from feeling icy, so the tree reads calm and cozy instead of cold. It is a beautiful choice for a bedroom or a neutral living room.

- **Ornaments:** [baby blue ball ornaments](https://link.amazon/B0cICxEwi) mixed with the same chocolate brown glass balls from the cherry cola tree, plus cream felt stars.
- **Ribbon:** cream velvet or a brown and blue gingham.
- **Topper:** a cream bow or a simple brass star.
- **Skirt:** a cream sherpa or chunky knit skirt.

## Light it so the colors glow

Color trees live or die by their lights. A few rules that work for all seven looks:

- **Use about 100 lights for every foot of tree.** That is roughly 600 to 700 for a 6-to-7-foot tree. Double it if you love a bright, magical look.
- **Choose warm white for color trees.** Burgundy, plum, emerald and brown all look richer under warm light. Connectable strands like this [100-count warm white LED string on green wire](https://link.amazon/B09RpdOc6) disappear into the branches.
- **Weave, don't wrap.** Start at the bottom near the trunk and run each strand out along a branch and back, so the tree glows from inside.
- **Light the room, too.** Turn off the overhead light and switch on one or two lamps with warm bulbs near the tree. Add candles on a side table or mantel for movement. For more on this, see our guide to [lighting for darker evenings](/posts/lighting-for-darker-evenings/).


## The order to decorate any tree

1. **Fluff every branch,** from the bottom up.
2. **Add the lights,** from the trunk out.
3. **Place the topper** before anything else goes on.
4. **Add ribbon or garland,** top to bottom.
5. **Hang the big ornaments,** some deep inside for depth.
6. **Fill in with smaller ones** in loose groups of three.
7. **Finish the base** with the skirt and wrapped gifts.
