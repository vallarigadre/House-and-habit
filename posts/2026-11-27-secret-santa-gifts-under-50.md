## How to make any Secret Santa gift feel thoughtful

A few small touches make an under-$50 gift look like you planned it for weeks.

1. **Add one consumable.** A packet of cocoa, a candy cane or a small bag of cookies tucked in with the gift makes it feel complete.
2. **Write a funny note.** One line in a card is enough. "From your Secret Santa, who definitely did not panic" always gets a laugh.
3. **Wrap it the same way as everyone else** if your group keeps it anonymous, so nobody can guess who it is from.
4. **Match the gift to one thing you know.** Always cold? Socks and a candle. Loves coffee? A mug. Bakes for the team? The apron.
